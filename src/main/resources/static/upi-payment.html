AuraPay · UPI payment reconciliation

For personal UPI QR transfers. This page records claims and supports manual reconciliation only.

Important: A UPI reference, screenshot, QR scan, or “I paid” message is not proof. Only mark a claim verified after independently confirming the matching credit in your bank/acquirer transaction records. This prototype never grants credits.

Submit a payment claim

First create a demo order in the AuraPay dashboard. Paste its order ID below. The order must match the exact amount.

Do not upload screenshots or share your UPI PIN/OTP. Reference is unverified and is used only to help you reconcile the bank record.

Admin review queue

Enter the secret configured as AURAPAY_QR_ADMIN_TOKEN in Render. It is sent only as a request header and is not stored by this page.
